What is Kaizen?

Kai (Change)

The action of changing or correcting. It implies that nothing is static and everything can be improved.

Zen (Good)

For the better. The goal is not just change, but change that leads to a better outcome.

Kaizen in DevOps

Small, Incremental Changes

Instead of massive overhauls, Kaizen encourages small, daily improvements in the CI/CD pipeline, code quality, and team collaboration.

Blameless Post-Mortems

Focus on process improvement rather than individual blame. Every failure is an opportunity to learn and prevent recurrence.

Eliminating Waste (Muda)

Streamlining workflows by removing manual toil, waiting times, and unnecessary context switching.

The Impact

Faster Time to Market
